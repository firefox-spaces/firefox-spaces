Import from Arc

Bring your Arc spaces over: pinned tabs, folders and today’s tabs, space by space. Nothing is sent anywhere — the file is read right here on your computer, and Arc itself isn’t touched or changed.

  1. Pick Arc’s sidebar file

    Arc keeps its sidebar in a file named StorableSidebar.json. Choose it below. On this computer it’s at:

    Drop the file here or

  2. Choose what to import

    “New profile” creates a fresh container with separate cookies — you’ll be logged out there until you sign in. Picking an existing profile keeps its logins.

    Your spaces use Arc profiles, but Arc keeps the profiles’ names in a second file. Add it to carry the names over — otherwise the profiles here will be called .

    Waiting for the file…

  3. Import

    Imported tabs start out asleep — they load the first time you click them in the sidebar, so importing hundreds of tabs costs nothing until you use them. Your new spaces are in the space switcher at the bottom of the sidebar. You can run this wizard again any time from ⚙ Settings; importing twice makes duplicates, though.
